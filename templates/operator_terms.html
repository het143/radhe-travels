Operator Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: April 2026 • Radhe Travels Network

1. Platform Commission & Fees

By registering as an operator on the Radhe Travels platform, you agree to the following financial structure:

  • Radhe Travels deducts a standard 10% Platform Commission on all gross ticket sales generated through the portal, unless a custom rate is explicitly granted by System Administration.
  • Commission is calculated automatically at the time of transaction.
  • Platform fees are non-refundable in the event of operator-initiated cancellations.

2. Fleet & Safety Standards

Operator guarantees that all vehicles registered on the platform meet minimum legal and safety standards:

  • All uploaded Fleet Registration Documents and Trade Licenses must be authentic, valid, and legally recognized by the government of India.
  • Vehicles must possess active commercial insurance and valid fitness certificates.
  • Falsifying documents or seating layouts is grounds for immediate, permanent account termination.

3. Payouts & Settlements

Settlements are processed according to the operator ledger:

  • Net Payable Amounts (Gross Revenue minus Platform Commission) are calculated in real-time on the operator dashboard.
  • Payouts are dispatched by System Administration on a scheduled basis to the registered business bank account.
  • Discrepancies in the ledger must be reported via the Customer Care module within 72 hours of settlement.

4. Liability & Insurance

Radhe Travels functions strictly as a technological aggregator and ticket booking platform. Radhe Travels assumes zero liability for physical accidents, luggage loss, mechanical breakdowns, or operational delays. The Fleet Operator holds 100% liability for the safe transport of passengers.
