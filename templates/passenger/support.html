Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my ticket and get a refund?

You can easily cancel your ticket by logging into your account, navigating to "My Journeys", and clicking the "Cancel Ticket" button next to your upcoming trip. Refunds are automatically processed with a 10% cancellation fee and will reflect in your account within 5-7 business days.

What is the luggage allowance per passenger?

Each passenger is allowed one standard piece of check-in luggage (up to 15 kg) and one small personal item (like a laptop bag or handbag) to be carried into the cabin. Extra luggage may be subject to additional charges at the boarding point.

Do I need a printed copy of my ticket?

No, Radhe Travels supports a 100% paperless boarding process. Simply show your e-ticket (from your email or the "My Journeys" dashboard) along with a valid Government ID to the conductor before boarding.
