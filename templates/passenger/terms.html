Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: March 2026 • Valid for the Gujarat Division Network

Welcome to Radhe Travels. By accessing our platform, booking a ticket, or utilizing our operator network, you agree to comply with the following Terms of Service. Please read them carefully.

1. Booking Policy & E-Tickets

Radhe Travels acts strictly as a technology provider connecting passengers with certified fleet operators. When you purchase a ticket:

  • Your seat is electronically locked upon successful payment.
  • The generated E-Ticket (PNR) must be presented along with a valid Government ID at the time of boarding.
  • Tickets are non-transferable. Passenger details provided during booking must match the traveler's ID.

2. Cancellation & Refund Rules

Refunds are processed automatically to the original payment method based on the time of cancellation relative to the departure time:

  • Before 24 Hours: 90% refund of the base fare (Platform fees are non-refundable).
  • 12 to 24 Hours: 50% refund of the base fare.
  • Less than 12 Hours: No refund will be issued.
  • In the rare event of a bus cancellation by the Operator, passengers will receive a 100% full refund within 3-5 business days.

3. Baggage Allowance

To ensure comfort and safety for all passengers, strict baggage limits are enforced by the operators:

  • Each passenger is allowed one piece of luggage up to 15 kg in the cargo hold.
  • One small personal cabin bag (e.g., laptop bag or purse) up to 5 kg is permitted inside the seating area.
  • Hazardous materials, flammables, weapons, and illicit substances are strictly prohibited.
  • Operators hold the right to deny boarding if luggage exceeds limits without prior approval.

4. Boarding Protocol

Punctuality is crucial for maintaining the network schedule.

  • Passengers must arrive at the designated boarding point at least 15 minutes before the scheduled departure.
  • Operators are not obligated to wait for late arrivals. Missing the bus constitutes a "No Show" and is non-refundable.
  • The Live Tracking link is provided for convenience, but traffic/network delays do not absolve the passenger of the arrival requirement.

5. Zero-Trust Privacy & Security

Your data is protected under our Zero-Trust architecture:

  • Payment information is tokenized and never stored on our local servers.
  • Passenger contact details are temporarily shared with the specific operator for boarding coordination and are masked post-journey.
  • Any attempt to manipulate the platform, bypass payment gateways, or submit fraudulent requests will result in an immediate hardware ban and legal action.
