Data Privacy Protocol

Your security and privacy are the foundation of the Radhe Travels experience.
Effective Date: April 2026

1. Data Collection Node

When you interact with the Radhe Travels ecosystem, we collect specific data nodes to ensure seamless bookings and secure transactions. This includes:

  • Identity Nodes: Full name, mobile number, and email address.
  • Financial Tokens: Encrypted transaction IDs. (Note: We do not store raw credit card or UPI pin data on our servers).
  • Telemetry: Device IP, browser type, and interaction logs for anti-fraud monitoring.

2. Processing & Usage

Your data acts as the fuel for your journey. We strictly utilize your information to generate E-Tickets, process refunds, send journey status alerts (via SMS/WhatsApp), and resolve disputes at our Customer Care Desk. We maintain a strict Zero-Sale policy—your data is never sold to third-party marketers.

3. Security & Encryption

All communications between your device and Radhe Travels are secured using military-grade TLS 1.3 encryption. Passwords are cryptographically hashed using the scrypt algorithm before entering our database, ensuring even root administrators cannot view your credentials.

4. Your Access Rights

Under the central Privacy Protocol, you retain full sovereignty over your data node:

  • Right to Access: You can view all your journey history via the "My Journeys" dashboard.
  • Right to Rectification: You can update your identity nodes (Name, Mobile) directly via your Profile.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request a complete purge of your account and booking history by opening a ticket at our Customer Care Desk.

For any inquiries regarding this protocol, please contact our Data Protection Officer via the Support Desk.
